# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

In the interest of fostering an open and welcoming environment, we as contributors and maintainers pledge to make participation in this project a harassment‑free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment for us include:

* Using welcoming and inclusive language
* Being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences
* Gracefully accepting constructive criticism
* Focusing on what is best for the community
* Showing empathy towards other community members

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

* The or demeaning language and personal attacks
* Harassing, trolling, or threatening comments or behavior
* Public or private harassment
* Publishing private information about a person without explicit permission
* Any other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported by contacting the project maintainers at **aminekhd@ptero.pro**. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly.

All maintainers are obligated to enforce this Code of Conduct in any forum associated with the project. This includes but is not limited to the issue tracker, pull‑request comments, chat channels, and any other public or private communications.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies both within the project’s public spaces and in private interactions when they are related to the project. The project maintainers may take appropriate action, including warning the offender, removing the offending content, or banning the offender from the project.
